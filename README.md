An intuitive, interactive, and beautifully designed course catalog that organizes and indexes **458+ free technical courses** from Egypt's **[MaharaTech (مهارة تك)](https://maharatech.gov.eg)** platform — powered by the **Information Technology Institute (ITI)**.

---

## 💡 Our Story & Purpose

### 🎯 The Inspiration
**MaharaTech** is one of the most valuable educational initiatives in the Arab world, offering hundreds of free, high-quality courses taught by industry leaders and ITI instructors. However, discovering the right course across deep category trees, finding courses with sign language support, or exploring all tracks in one place was difficult.

### 🚀 The Mission
We built this project to solve that problem:
- **Centralize & Organize**: Bring together every single active course on MaharaTech into one unified, fast, and easy-to-explore directory.
- **Support Inclusivity**: Put a spotlight on courses available in **Arabic Sign Language (لغة الإشارة)** so deaf and hard-of-hearing learners can find accessible content effortlessly.
- **Empower Students & Learners**: Help students, self-taught developers, and tech enthusiasts quickly find what they want to learn and jump straight into their educational journey.

- Type any keyword, track name, or topic to see matching courses in real time.

- Explore everything from **Artificial Intelligence**, **Cybersecurity**, and **Software Engineering** to **Digital Arts**, **Game Development**, and **Cloud Computing**.

- **Grid View**: Visual cards with course banners and tags.
- **Track View**: Neatly grouped by topic and specialization.
- **List View**: A compact, high-speed list for quick scanning.

### ⚡ Ready Everywhere, Instantly
- Opens directly in any browser on your computer, tablet, or phone.
- No sign-ups, no installation, and no technical setup required.

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. Direct Web Browsing
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Start exploring, searching, and clicking **"Open Course →"** to start learning immediately on the official MaharaTech platform!

### 2. Fast Navigation Tips
- **Search Anything**: Click the search box or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to search courses instantly.
- **Sign Language Toggle**: Click the **Sign Language** switch at the top to filter for accessible courses.
- **Sidebar Categories**: Click on any track in the left sidebar to expand and explore its sub-topics.
